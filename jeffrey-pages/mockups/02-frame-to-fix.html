Ten skills that know
how to read a JVM.

The Microscope plugin is one package for Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI. Skills carry the method; three sub-agents do the heavy reading and hand back findings, never raw dumps.

i.

profile-run

Record a benchmark, a test or the app — then analyze what comes out.

ii.

analyze-jfr

CPU, wall-clock, allocation, locks, GC, JIT, threads, native memory.

iii.

analyze-heap

What holds the memory, what leaks, which class loader survived.

iv.

advise-jfr

Hot frames mapped to source, minimal edits applied on request.

v.

regression-check

Two revisions profiled the same way, weighed against each other.

Plus compare-jfr, analyze-hub, jfr-sql, heap-sql, report — and agents profile-lead, profile-analyst, heap-triage.

for the rest of us.
