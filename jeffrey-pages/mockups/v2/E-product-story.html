01 · Analyze

Deep analysis that finally renders fast.

Drop in a JFR file or a heap dump, or pull one from a Hub. Microscope runs on your laptop as a JAR or a container.

  • Flamegraphs and differential flamegraphs for every JFR event with a stack trace
  • Sub-second timelines — zoom into the millisecond it stalled
  • Heap dumps: dominator tree, leak suspects, OQL
  • GC, JIT, threads, HTTP, JDBC dashboards and traces

Collect runtime data
from running services.

When the recording lives in production: a containerised collector for Kubernetes that captures JFR recordings, heap dumps and logs — and serves them over gRPC to Microscope and your agent.

  • Runs as a container in Kubernetes
  • Collects via shared volume — zero agent overhead
  • Serves a whole session, or the hour that matters
