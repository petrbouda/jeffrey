3Dock — the same panel exceptions already use

Stop having two floating panels in one component. A throw already opens .exc-dock: an inset strip over the rows, a --color-light header carrying a badge, a select button and a close, and a body that scrolls. A notification gets the identical panel — the two are already driven by the same openEntryId ref, so only one can be open at a time, which makes this one surface serving both instant families rather than two made to look alike.

Updated. Gutters go 6rem → 2.5rem, and the extra width is spent rather than absorbed: the attributes are now the span detail's own .sd-region + .sd-table.is-attr — the same key/value table TraceSpanEvent.attributes is drawn with two panels away — and the message keeps a 62ch measure beside them instead of stretching to 900px. Two consequences worth naming: the 6-attribute cap goes (MAX_POPOVER_ATTRIBUTES exists because a 22rem × 13rem box could not hold more; a scrolling table can, and truncating a notification's own detail when there is room is just data withheld), and the unlabelled .pop-meta row — the audit's "PROFILE with nothing saying it is the category" — becomes a .sd-block, formatted exactly as the span detail's Span · identity: a dotted uppercase label over a plain .sd-table, no frame. That is the distinction the span detail already draws — a region is for a recorded map the panel does not otherwise show, a block is for facts qualifying what it already names — so the attributes stay framed and the identity does not.
