4Inline region — nothing floats

The component's own primary detail idiom is not a popover: clicking a span opens TraceSpanInlineDetail as the next row, on the argument that "a panel at the bottom scrolls the bar that was clicked out of view". This applies the same rule to notifications — the entry opens as a .sd-region block in the rows column, the literal same markup the span detail uses for its Notifications region. Nothing overlays anything, so no z-index, no clipping, no translateX(-70%), no close-on-outside question. The cost is the one the popover was invented to avoid: opening it pushes the span rows down, so the bar you were looking at moves.
