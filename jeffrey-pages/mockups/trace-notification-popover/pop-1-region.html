1Region shell — same size, house chrome

Smallest possible change for the most consistency. The popover keeps its 22rem footprint and its anchor to the mark; what it gains is the shell that TraceSpanInlineDetail already uses for this exact notification — a tinted header band in the severity colour (color-mix(--mark 8%), uppercase 0.07em), the offset pushed right in mono like .sd-src, a close button borrowed from the exception dock, and the body padded at .sd-region-body scale instead of 0.55rem 0.7rem. The chips and the severity word are untouched: they are already byte-for-byte the region's own rules. So the same notification now looks the same in both places the reader meets it.
