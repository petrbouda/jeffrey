The rail's cross already has a popover. It gains the top frames — the throwing line and the three above it — so the reader gets the where without opening the span, without leaving the timeline, and without a click. A "full stack" line hands off to whichever of the other directions is built for the whole thing, so this composes with any of them rather than competing.

What it buys

The answer arrives on hover. Nine times out of ten the reader wants one line — which of their own methods threw — and this is the only direction that delivers it without a click. It also works from the rail, which folding and critical-path-only cannot hide.

What it costs

A preview is all it can ever be. The popover is capped at 13rem with internal scroll (that cap was added because it had nowhere to grow), so 45 frames will not fit and should not. On its own this is not a stack-trace feature; it is a good front door for one, and something else has to be the room behind it.
