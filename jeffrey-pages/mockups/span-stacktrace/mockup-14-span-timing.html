3 Which size, against the kind chip

The span-kind chip is size="xs"; the metric rows this borrows from are size="s". Both drawn here so the pairing can be looked at rather than argued about. s is the proposal — a measurement you read is not a label you glance at, and the step in size is what says so.

What the removals cost, and what the badge quietly keeps

The critical path. Whether a span is on it stays visible in the waterfall — rows carry a critical class, the legend has an on the critical path swatch, and the toolbar has a Critical path only filter. What goes is how much of the span was on the path and what share of the trace that was, and the off-state sentence “not on the critical path — it ran beside work that outlasted it”.

The children count keeps more than it looks. The line it replaces said three different things — “a leaf — nothing ran inside it”, “7 children, all on other threads”, or plain “7 children” — and its comment explains the middle case exists because a full green bar would otherwise read as a leaf. The badge still separates them: Children: 0 is a leaf, and Children: 7 beside a legend reading all of it its own says the children ran somewhere else. The prose goes; the distinction does not.

The panel does not get shorter, which I assumed last round until measuring it: the drawings above are the same height. .sd-cols is a grid taking its height from the identity table’s five rows, not from the timing column — so two lines come out of a column that had room to spare. What is gained is a head that states both numbers in the app’s own vocabulary, and a timing block that is only the meter.
