All five directions

2List left, stack right

The Exceptions region becomes two columns. The throws stay a compact list on the left — class, message, offset — and the selected one's stack fills a pane on the right that scrolls on its own. The region's height stops depending on how deep the stack is, which is the whole point: switching between three throws never moves anything except the right-hand pane.

What it buys

Built for a span with several throws. Comparing where two of them came from is one click, not two expand-and-scroll cycles, and the list stays legible while you do it. The region has a fixed height whatever the stack depth, so nothing below it ever jumps.

What it costs

It spends horizontal room the panel does not have. The detail already carries an event-fields table; at a realistic modal width the left column squeezes the class names down to their simple names, which is exactly the information a reader scans by. It also earns nothing on the common case — a span with a single throw shows an empty-feeling list beside one stack.
