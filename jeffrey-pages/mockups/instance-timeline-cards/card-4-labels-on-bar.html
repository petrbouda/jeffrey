4Labels on the bar — the meta strip disappears

The meta strip exists to say started, finished, duration. But the bar already is those three facts — its left edge is the start, its right edge is the end, its width is the duration. So this variant deletes the strip and puts the numbers where they already are: timestamps stamped at the bar's two ends, duration inside the bar. The lane grows to make room, the card loses a row, and the timeline stops being a decorative echo of the text above it. A pulsing dot marks the live edge of a running instance.
