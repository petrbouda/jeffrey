3Two-column — identity left, timeline right

The card stops being three stacked bands and becomes two columns: everything that identifies the instance on the left in a fixed-width column, the lane filling the rest on the right, both vertically centred. The fixed left column is the point — it is what makes every bar start at the same x across every card, which is the thing the current page cannot do without giving up the card. You keep the card border, the status edge, the chips and the drawer; you just stop spending a full-width row on the name and another on the meta.
