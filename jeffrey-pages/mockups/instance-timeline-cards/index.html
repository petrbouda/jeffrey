Five refinements of the current card

Same page, same card, same three instances at 24H. Nothing here is re-architected into a waterfall, a table or a split pane — every variant keeps the card-per-instance layout, the coloured status edge, the session chip, the chevron and the drawer. What changes is how the card is arranged and how tall it is.

Two things are fixed in all five, because they are defects rather than taste: time now runs left → right (oldest at the left, Now at the right), and the ruler is drawn once above the stack instead of once per card — which is what lets the three bars be compared to each other, and immediately shows the thing this screen exists to show: qb9g7 ends and 25pjc begins at the same instant. Also gone everywhere: the clipped edge labels, the "adjacent finished / adjacent active" legend (it described array parity, not data), and the silent empty card for an instance outside the window.

Minimal fix 91px

The card is untouched — tint, three zones, everything. Only the ruler moves out and the axis flips. Lowest-risk option: if you like the card and only hate the timeline part, this is the whole fix.

Compact 78px

The meta strip folds up into the header line as start → end plus a duration pill. Two rows per card instead of four, with nothing removed but four words of chrome.

Identity in a fixed-width column on the left, lane filling the right, both vertically centred. The fixed column is what makes every bar start at the same x — the shared-axis benefit without leaving the card.
4

Labels on the bar 77px

The bar already is started/finished/duration, so the meta strip is deleted and the numbers are stamped on the bar's ends and inside it. The lane becomes the card's main object.
5

Refined 88px

Structurally identical to today; every change is styling. The tint goes, the box icon becomes a status dot, the meta gets a real hierarchy, and session boundaries become hairlines inside the bar.

What every variant fixes, regardless of which you pick

  • Axis direction. left = (now − t) / rangeMs becomes left = (t − windowStart) / rangeMs, plus the pull-back clamp so a floored width cannot push a bar past the right edge.
  • One ruler. .time-axis comes out of .instance-card-body and is rendered once above .card-stack, inset to match the lane.
  • Clipped labels. translateX(-50%) on every tick half-hangs the first and last off both edges; the endpoints get pinned instead.
  • The legend. sessionBarClass's strong/light alternation is index parity, so the legend explains an implementation detail. Both go.
  • The empty card. Math.min(width, 100 − left) collapses an out-of-window instance to 0%. It now says how far outside it is.
  • Colour drift. The card washes use rgba(16,185,129,…) and rgba(156,163,175,…) — #10b981 and #9ca3af — against --color-success #00d27a and --color-text-light #b6c1d2, so the tints never matched the accent borders next to them. 20 raw rgba() in total.
