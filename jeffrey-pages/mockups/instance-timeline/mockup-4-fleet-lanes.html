4Fleet lanes

The Trace waterfall puts GC pause and Safepoint in lanes above the spans, and washes their columns down behind every row. The same move here: three aggregate lanes above the instances — Instances running, Crash loops, and Deploys. The deploy boundaries are not new data: the pod names already carry the replicaset hash, so 5c4b1e77 → 86dd87c8ff → 74bd9b8c8d is derivable today and drawn as a wash straight down the page. The payoff is the question no per-instance row can answer — how many were up at once.
