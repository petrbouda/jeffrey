5Lifecycle pins on the bar

Today an instance bar is a featureless slab: it says when and says nothing about what happened. The Trace waterfall solved the identical problem for notifications and exceptions by drawing each instant twice — once on a rail above the rows that survives folding and filtering, once as a pin on the bar that owns it, positioned against the track so the two always share an x. Applied here, the pins are: instance start (circle), deploy (diamond), crash (cross), and stop (bar) carrying the jdk.Shutdown reason — which the app already fetches for the drawer and shows nowhere else. Shape carries the family, so the colour ramp can safely reuse red.
