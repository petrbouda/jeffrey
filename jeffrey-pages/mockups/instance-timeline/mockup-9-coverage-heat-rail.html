9Coverage heat rail

At 7D and 30D a proportional bar stops working: an hour is 0.14% of a month, so every short instance collapses into the same 2-pixel sliver and the picture becomes a row of dashes. This swaps the representation at long ranges the way traceTimelineBuckets.ts already does for traces — bucket the window and shade one cell per bucket by how much of it was recorded. Density stops depending on duration, so a 37-minute crash-loop is as visible as a four-day run. Meant to pair with direction 1 or 2, not replace them: the app picks bars under 24 hours and cells above.
