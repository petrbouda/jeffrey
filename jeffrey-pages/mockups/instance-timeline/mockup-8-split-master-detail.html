8Split master/detail

The drawer you like has one structural problem: it opens inside the list, so everything below jumps down and the instance you were comparing against scrolls off. This splits the page instead — a compact instance list on the left with a persistent selection, and the detail on the right, which holds the selected instance's session waterfall plus the environment cards. Nothing moves when you change selection, so you can walk the list with the keyboard and watch the right pane change. The detail content is exactly today's drawer, unedited — only its address changed.
