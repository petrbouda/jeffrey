6Uptime vs recording coverage

The span waterfall encodes two things in one bar: the pale body is the span's whole duration, and the solid stretches are its own work, drawn where it actually happened. The same shape answers a question this page cannot currently ask — was the JVM actually being recorded the whole time it was up? Pale body = the instance's lifetime, solid = time covered by a recording session, and the holes between them are blind spots. Today those are invisible: a session bar is drawn, so the row looks covered.
